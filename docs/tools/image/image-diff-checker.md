## 🧠 What is this, in plain English?

Places two images side by side (or overlaid) and highlights exactly which pixels differ between them — useful for visual regression testing or spotting subtle changes between two versions of a design or screenshot.

## 🙋 Who is this for, and when do I need it?

- You're checking whether a UI change accidentally broke the visual layout of a page ("visual regression testing").
- You want to compare two photos or design exports to spot small differences.

## ✨ What it can do

- High-performance, 100% browser-based HTML5 canvas image processing for Side-by-Side Image Comparator.
- Zero file uploads: your photos, screenshots, and graphics never leave your local device.
- Precision layout, dimensions, compression quality, and format controls (PNG, JPEG, WebP).
- Built-in privacy safeguards: strip sensitive GPS and camera EXIF metadata in seconds.
- Instant live preview with fast one-click download in full resolution.

1. Upload or drag-and-drop your image files directly into the browser tool workspace.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes & troubleshooting

- Comparing two images of different pixel dimensions will produce a mostly-red "different everywhere" result — resize both images to match first for a meaningful comparison.
